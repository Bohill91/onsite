ONSITE SUB-CONTRACTOR REFERRAL PROGRAMME

Earn up to £100 per qualifying referral.

Refer a CIS sub-contractor to OnSite. When they complete 5 paid days through OnSite, you earn £50. When they reach 20 paid days, you earn another £50. The referred sub-contractor also earns £25 after their first 5 paid days.

To receive referral rewards, you must complete your OnSite account setup and pass CIS verification. You do not need to complete paid work yourself to earn referral rewards.

Twenty qualifying referrals could earn you up to £2,000 in referral rewards. Registration alone does not qualify for a reward.
