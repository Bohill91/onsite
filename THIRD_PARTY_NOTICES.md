# Third-Party Data Notices

## GeoNames

The OnSite UK settlement dataset is derived from the GeoNames `cities500` and
Great Britain alternate-name exports downloaded on 19 August 2026.

- Source: https://www.geonames.org/
- Data downloads: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
- Licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The derived dataset keeps only populated-place and administrative-seat records
for England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. GeoNames supplies the source
data as-is without a warranty of accuracy, timeliness or completeness.
