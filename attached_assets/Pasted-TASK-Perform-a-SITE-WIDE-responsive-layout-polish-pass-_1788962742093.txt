==================================================
2. GOAL
==================================================

OnSite should respond deliberately as the viewport narrows.

It must NOT look like a desktop layout being squeezed until words randomly wrap.

At every supported width:

- labels should remain readable
- buttons should not split words
- navigation should stay aligned
- cards should adapt intentionally
- field rows should stack when necessary
- no horizontal overflow
- no clipped content
- no overlapping controls
- no awkward half-responsive states

The app should feel intentionally designed at every width.
